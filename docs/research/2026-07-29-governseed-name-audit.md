# GovernSeed Name Audit

Checked: 2026-07-29 (Asia/Taipei)

Status: **CLEAR_FOR_OPEN_SOURCE_BRANDING**

This is a preliminary open-source branding screen, not legal trademark
clearance, an opinion on registrability, or a reservation of any name. No
direct collision with an AI-agent governance product was found. The user
completed the previously requested manual EUIPO/TMview check and supplied a
captured `contains GovernSeed` result with no rows. For this project's narrow
direct-collision screen, that broader character-match result also excludes an
exact `GovernSeed` row from the displayed result set. It does not replace an
exact-mode, similarity, common-law, or counsel-led clearance search.

`GovSeed` is not an approved abbreviation. It would introduce avoidable
government and seed-accelerator ambiguity.

## Method and Interpretation

Public, unauthenticated result surfaces were used where possible. A `404`,
zero-result response, NXDOMAIN, or absent search result means only that no
public object was returned at the checked time. It does not reserve a name,
prove publish rights, exclude private or unindexed objects, or guarantee
registrar availability.

Collision levels in this document are:

- `none`: no collision was observed on the checked surface;
- `remote`: the exact character sequence appeared outside the product category
  or as a non-brand token;
- `adjacent`: a related category or similar-looking name exists without an
  exact same-domain brand collision;
- `direct`: an exact or confusingly close brand exists in the same product
  domain.
